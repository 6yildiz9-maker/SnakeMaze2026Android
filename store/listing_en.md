## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
Relive the golden era of handheld mobile gaming with **Pixelfang: Snake Maze** — the definitive retro Nokia snake experience reinvented for modern touchscreens!

Featuring authentic green monochrome LCD aesthetics, dot-matrix scanline effects, tactile chiptune sound effects, and ergonomic retro physical-style controls.

---

### 🎮 GAME MODES
* **Maze Mode (Dynamic Obstacles):** As your score grows, 2x2 obstacles materialize on the grid, demanding precision steering and fast reflexes!
* **Classic Mode (No Obstacles):** Pure nostalgic snake with wrapping borders and zero obstacles. Just you, the snake, and the food.
* **Time Attack (60 Seconds):** High-stakes rush! Race against the clock to collect as much food as possible. Feel the tension with the 10-second countdown alert!
* **Survival Mode:** High initial speed, relentless obstacle pacing, and zero room for error. Only for true masters.
* **Custom Maze Editor:** Draw and save your own obstacle courses on a retro 16x16 grid!

---

### 🍎 SPECIAL FOOD TYPES
* **Regular Food (+1 pt):** Classic nourishment to extend your tail.
* **Bonus Food (+3 pts, +2 length):** High-value bonus fruit with a live countdown timer.
* **Golden Apple (+5 pts):** Rare, delicious jackpot food.
* **Ice Berry:** Chill out! Temporarily slows your snake for easier navigation.
* **Poison Mushroom (-1 pt):** Watch out — shrinks your score and tests your avoidance!
* **Mystery Egg:** Wildcard effect — could be a massive score boost or an unexpected speed shift!

---

### 🕹️ CONTROL SCHEMES TAILORED FOR YOU
* **Classic 2-Button Zig-Zag:** Original Nokia 3310 style — tap Left or Right to turn relative to your snake's heading.
* **D-Pad 4-Way Controls:** Dedicated Up, Down, Left, and Right buttons with configurable button size (Small, Medium, Large) and Left/Right hand layouts.
* **Smooth Swipe & Drag:** Swipe anywhere on screen for intuitive touch gestures.
* **Full Keyboard Support:** Desktop arrow keys and WASD keys with Spacebar to pause.

---

### 🏆 CAREER, STATS & ACHIEVEMENTS
* **Ranks from Worm to Mythical Leviathan:** Rise through 7 distinct skill ranks.
* **25 Challenging Achievements:** Wall Phobia, Narrow Passer, Ouroboros, Light Speed, and more!
* **Daily Quests:** Fresh daily challenges to test your skills and earn trophies.
* **Career Analytics:** Track your longest snake, total food eaten, session durations, and high scores per mode.
