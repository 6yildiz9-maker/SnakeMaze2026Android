# Privacy Policy — Snake Maze 2026

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026

Snake Maze 2026 ("we", "our", or "app") is a retro snake game designed for casual fun. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Local Game Data:** High scores, career statistics, achievement unlocks, sound/theme settings, and custom mazes are stored locally on your device (`localStorage` / device Preferences). This data never leaves your device.
- **Advertising Data:** On mobile devices (Google Play version), Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers (AD_ID) and approximate IP location to deliver rewarded video ads.

## 2. Children's Privacy
The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 3. Contact
If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer at `support@snakemaze2026.app`.
