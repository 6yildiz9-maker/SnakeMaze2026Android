# Privacy Policy — Pixelfang: Snake Maze

**Effective Date:** November 9, 2026

Pixelfang: Snake Maze ("we", "our", or "app") is a retro snake game designed for casual fun. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

## 1. Information and Services
- **Local Game Data:** High scores, career statistics, achievement unlocks, sound/theme settings, and custom mazes are stored on your device in Android Preferences. The game developer does not upload this game data to a server.
- **Advertising:** The Google Play version uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded video and interstitial ads. Google may collect and process advertising/device identifiers, IP address (which can be used to infer approximate location), ad interactions, and diagnostics under Google's privacy terms. The app requests consent with Google's User Messaging Platform where required and provides an Ad Privacy Options entry in Android settings.
- **Network and offline use:** Core gameplay works offline after installation. Ad requests require an internet connection.

## 2. Children's Privacy
The app does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
